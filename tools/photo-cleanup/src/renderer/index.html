Photo Cleanup

Drop a photo here JPEG, PNG or WebP (also AVIF, BMP, GIF), up to 60 megapixels

Tap something to select it, or paint over it, and it is removed and the gap filled in to match the surroundings. You can also make the photo wider or taller and let the new edges be filled in. Everything runs on this computer, offline; nothing is uploaded. Your original file is never changed.
