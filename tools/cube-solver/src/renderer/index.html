How to hold the cube

Hold the cube with the white centre on top and the green centre facing you, and keep it that way. The centres never move on a 3x3, so they are already filled in. Fill in the front, then turn the whole cube a quarter to the left for the right face, again for the back and the left face, always with the same colour on top. For the top face, tip the cube towards you (the back goes up); for the bottom, tip it away from you (the front goes up).
