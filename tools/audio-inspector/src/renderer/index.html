Audio Inspector

what is really inside
Drop audio files or a folder here FLAC, WAV, AIFF, ALAC, APE, WavPack, MP3, AAC, Ogg, Opus and more

Each file is listened to on this computer, offline. You get a plain answer: real lossless, fake lossless (a FLAC or WAV made from an MP3), upsampled, or can't tell; plus the true sample rate and bits, loudness, peaks, clipping and a spectrogram. Your files are only read, never changed.

Click a file to see its spectrogram and all the numbers.
